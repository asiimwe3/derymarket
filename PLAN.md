# Roadmap — 33 features, 6 phases

Progress: 0/33 complete

## Phase 1 — Core Trade Infrastructure (0/6)
- [ ] Unified accounts, identities and roles
- [ ] Trade Engine and transaction lifecycle
- [ ] Catalog, listings, pricing and inventory
- [ ] Payment integration and financial ledger
- [ ] Order management, delivery and refunds
- [ ] Trust, verification, disputes and admin
Exit: first real order completes end-to-end.

## Phase 2 — Market and Deals (0/5)
- [ ] B2C retail marketplace
- [ ] C2C classifieds
- [ ] Chat, offers and negotiation
- [ ] Ratings, returns and promoted listings
- [ ] Mobile app and offline-conscious workflows
Exit: public B2C + C2C launch.

## Phase 3 — Business and Wholesale (0/5)
- [ ] B2B and B2B2B wholesale
- [ ] C2B and D2C commerce
- [ ] RFQs, quotations and purchase orders
- [ ] Subscriptions, contracts and procurement
- [ ] Business dashboards and accounting integrations
Exit: first B2B PO completed with escrow milestones.

## Phase 4 — Extended Trade (0/6)
- [ ] Services marketplace and bookings
- [ ] Agricultural trade
- [ ] Auctions and bidding
- [ ] Rental and leasing
- [ ] Barter and consignment
- [ ] Dropshipping and recurring orders
Exit: two new trade modes live with real transactions.

## Phase 5 — Global and Institutional Trade (0/5)
- [ ] Import and export management
- [ ] Ship From Abroad and consolidation hubs
- [ ] Customs, freight and landed-cost calculations
- [ ] B2G procurement and institutional workflows
- [ ] Multi-country and multi-currency support
Exit: first consolidated shipment cleared and delivered.

## Phase 6 — Advanced Trade Ecosystem (0/6)

- [ ] Advanced business reporting
- [ ] Loyalty, referrals and affiliate tools
- [ ] Digital commerce
- [ ] Financial service integrations (subject to approval)
